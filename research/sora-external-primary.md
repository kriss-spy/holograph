## Audited coappearances without a unit claim

[Nomu’s 23 November 2018 cover](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=35fKzdDH654) credits Suntory Nomu, Fuji Aoi and Sora. The inspected title and description establish a complete musical collaboration but no shared unit name. Secondary catalog label ときの & 燦鳥 is not promoted into a named duo, especially from this three-person performance.

[Sora’s 1 June 2021 Among Us stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zkDN1ZTppy8) uses the event tag #親族宇宙人狼 and credits Sora plus Asano Ruri, AZKi, Else, Kaname Mahiro, Kanade Mimi, Ginga Alice, Hibiki Ao, Fuji Aoi and Yozora Mel. It explicitly calls Else/Mimi organizers and provides performer links. This proves the event lineup, not a persistent unit or literal family ties; it remains a documented event outside canonical units. Episode and concert guests are likewise not appended to the April Fools quartet.

The candidate ledger retains these exclusions so future work does not mistake them for uninspected missing units. This is a bounded audit of discovered leads, not exhaustive external collaboration coverage.

## Hoshimatic Project historical membership

[The official launch merchandise page](https://shop.hololivepro.com/products/hoshimaticproject) calls this a Suisei-led dance-unit project and lists ten performer-specific stand variants: Sora, Suisei, Aki, Matsuri, Subaru, Towa, Nene, Koyori, Chloe and Iroha. The printed sale start is 30 December 2023. [The 2026 project merchandise](https://shop.hololivepro.com/products/hoshimaticproject_2026) lists nine corresponding performers, excluding Chloe. [The official BEEP BEEP CD description](https://shop.hololivepro.com/products/hoshimaticproject_beepbeep) expressly calls all nine named singers members of the unit. The 2026 pages give shipping years, but their exact publication dates were not established; the audit does not invent sale/release dates. Two dated lineup snapshots preserve Chloe historically and do not establish a precise membership-change date. These are named-project membership records, not generic song credits turned into a unit.

[The original Sora/Machita Chima question stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0lehGReyK8M), dated 7 December 2025, explicitly prints #ぬんぬんまちた. Sora’s custom guest heading names Chima and links her performer handle. The [official Nijisanji profile](https://www.nijisanji.jp/talents/l/chima-machita) supplies her external identity. [Target watch metadata](sora-chima-primary-metadata.json) was independently extracted; the secondary index was only a discovery route. Nunnun Machita is a reading aid.
