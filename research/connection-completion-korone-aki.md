Researched 2026-10-06 for GitHub issues [#3](https://github.com/kriss-spy/holograph/issues/3) and [#7](https://github.com/kriss-spy/holograph/issues/7). Issue bodies were read with `gh issue view`. Web fetches intermittently failed, so direct public page HTML and its rendered `ytInitialData` fields supplied primary evidence. No video playback or spoken claims were asserted as verified.

English labels are transliterations of the primary Japanese spelling; Japanese aliases are retained. They do not establish an official English spelling. `Yakamashii musume`, `ShionPan`, `InuTakaShishiLamb`, and `Kamichamarose` in feedback are leads; primary spellings support やかまし娘, 塩んぱん, いぬたかししらむ, かみちゃまろーぜ respectively. None are declared inactive merely because a dated archive is old. The AnPonTan/Robotaru page date can differ by one day from Japanese event listings; month precision avoids asserting a timezone conversion without a start timestamp.
