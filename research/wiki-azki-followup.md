# AZKi deferred-name follow-up — issues #5 / #10

This is additional evidence, not an assertion that all collaboration names or their origin dates have been verified.

## Method and scope

[AZKi’s Seesaa profile](https://seesaawiki.jp/hololivetv/d/AZKi), especially “コンビ、ユニット関連”, supplied spellings and complete proposed lineups. It names あずみぉーん, かなあず, わたあず, こよあず, さかずき, あずいろ, AZRyS, AZBae, あずのせ, RiONAZKi and RoARiS. This wiki is discovery evidence only. Some previously supplied Latin spellings are reader labels; do not claim them to be official aliases without primary evidence. Direct wiki HTTPS returned 403, while the web reader retrieved the page; this does not invalidate the independently retrievable YouTube evidence.

Followed song/stream and video index leads, then fetched each YouTube watch page with `hl=ja`. Read the target watch page’s `ytInitialData.videoPrimaryInfoRenderer` title and date and `videoSecondaryInfoRenderer` uploader and attributed description. These are the target uploader’s metadata, not recommended-video titles, viewer comments, or secondary reproductions. Relevant uploader handles/tags and evidence summaries retained in [wiki-azki-primary-metadata.json](wiki-azki-primary-metadata.json). Dates below are YouTube’s Japanese displayed dates, not independently reconstructed timestamps. No spoken naming timestamps were verified in this pass.

The evidence verifies performer-used named collaboration snapshots. It does not establish official company unit status, ongoing activity, first naming/formation date, friendship, or inactivity. Retain former/alumni participants in dated historical records.
