## Evidence and limits

SoraMiko is established as early as the official ON STAGE! release credit (2020-10-21); the 2024 and 2026 original mathematics titles independently reuse そらみこ with Sakura Miko explicitly listed. IoSora’s original 2025-02-09 Human Fall Flat title prints イオそら and its description identifies @AiraniIofifteen. SoraChoco’s 2023-09-19 original title prints そらちょこ and description names 癒月ちょこ / @YuzukiChoco. These are source-supported collaboration names.

ぬんぬんばんちょー explicitly declares the hashtag in Sora’s own description, then names Hajime. そらわたあずぽる explicitly prints the four-person hashtag, with AZKi, Watame and Polka all named in the title and guest list. Preserve the entire quartet. 夜空の時 is a historical Mel duo, independently visible in Sora’s still-accessible original title and guest description.

SoraRio is a composite: Riona’s performer-owned short prints そらリオ and the Victor-provided original recording credits Tokino Sora featuring Riona, released 2026-08-05. Generic music credits alone were not treated as a named group.

アキそら’s title also prints AKISORA. AppleSoda / アップルソーダ remains a distinct unfinished alias lead from the fan dictionary; do not silently import it as a primary alias. This does not block the fully verified duo.

Secondary discovery was used only to locate originals. VTH references its Seesaa index and is not independent primary verification. Additional international collaborations and external performer pairs, including ぬんぬんまちた, are handled in the parent’s separate audit. The results are a documented improvement, not an exhaustive inventory of every Sora coappearance.
