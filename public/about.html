About this atlas

An independent fan research project exploring publicly documented Hololive collaborations, named duos and units. It is not affiliated with COVER Corp.

Research coverage

The selected roster contains 80 talents, including alumni, former members and affiliates. The roster snapshot is dated 3 October 2026; historical roster records were updated on 6 October 2026 and relationship evidence on 7 October 2026. The dataset contains 127 relationship records backed by 235 primary source records. Two records describe different dated casts of holoWitches.

This is a researched selection. Missing ties mean incomplete research, not an absence of relationships. Current activity is not implied by a talent being listed.

  • Mel and Rushia retain historical cohort membership with contract-termination dates. Include former members controls their visibility along with alumni.
  • Fubuki belongs to both Gen 1 and GAMERS. Selected cohort views place her in that cohort; the overview retains one identity with both affiliations. Council, Project: HOPE and Promise have related-cohort navigation while preserving historical membership: Sana remains in Council.
  • Gen 1 includes the evidenced Natsuiro Fubuki collaboration.
  • Gen 3 includes six documented pairs and a partial Shiraken unit. A partial unit retains its complete membership in the source panel.
  • Generation membership, music credits and proximity on the graph do not imply friendship or romance.
  • A multi-member unit is shown through a unit node; it does not create a friendship between every pair.
  • Dates describe the documented evidence. Historical collaborations remain visible without implying continuing activity.

Using the map

Choose a cohort, talent, unit or topic. Automatic layout uses fCoSE to spread connected clusters; circular and cohort-grid alternatives are available. Dense graphs can still have crossings. Select a connection for its sources, or use the searchable directory for keyboard access. Fullscreen provides more room; its Details button opens the source panel.
